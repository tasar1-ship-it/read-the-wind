Rule 11: when boats are on the same tack and overlapped, a windward boat keeps clear of a leeward boat. Preamble to Part 2 Section C: the mark rules do not apply at a starting mark surrounded by navigable water, from the time the boats are approaching it to start until they have left it astern.

S3 World Sailing, The Racing Rules of Sailing for 2025-2028, with changes and corrections to April 2026. Rule 26, Starting races: the warning signal five minutes before the starting signal, the preparatory signal four minutes before, the one-minute signal, then the starting signal.

S4 World Sailing, The Racing Rules of Sailing for 2025-2028, with changes and corrections to April 2026. Rule 29, Recalls: flag X with one sound for an individual recall, the First Substitute with two sounds for a general recall; rule 29.1 does not apply when the U flag or the black flag applies. Rule 30, Starting penalties: the I flag, the Z flag, the U flag and the black flag, each applying in the last minute before the starting signal. Z: the scoring penalty. U: disqualified without a hearing, unless the race is restarted. Black: disqualified without a hearing even if the race is restarted, with the sail number displayed before the next warning signal. Rule 21.1 (rule 22.1 in the previous book): a boat sailing back to the pre-start side after the starting signal keeps clear of boats that are not. The definition Start: the hull entirely on the pre-start side, then crossing the line. Rule 44.3(c): when the notice of race or sailing instructions state no number of points, the scoring penalty is twenty per cent of the score for Did Not Finish, rounded to the nearest tenth of a point, and it cannot make a boat's score worse than that score. Appendix A: under the low point system first place scores one point, second two, and so on; a boat that did not finish, or was over the line and did not return, scores one more than the number of boats entered in the series. S5 World Sailing, The Racing Rules of Sailing for 2025-2028, with changes and corrections to April 2026. Rule 13: after a boat passes head to wind she keeps clear of other boats until she is on a close-hauled course. Rule 15: a boat that acquires right of way must at first give the other boat room to keep clear, unless she acquires it because of the other boat's actions. Rule 16.2: on a beat to windward, when a port-tack boat is keeping clear by sailing to pass to leeward of a starboard-tack boat, the starboard-tack boat does not bear away if the port-tack boat would then have to change course at once to keep clear. S6 World Sailing, The Racing Rules of Sailing for 2025-2028, with changes and corrections to April 2026. Definitions: the zone is the area within three hull lengths of a mark; a boat is fetching a mark when she can pass to windward of it and leave it on the required side without changing tack; mark-room is room for a boat to leave a mark on the required side, to sail to the mark when her proper course is to sail close to it, and to round the mark as needed to sail the course; boats are overlapped when neither is clear astern of the other. Rule 18.1: the mark rules apply between boats required to leave a mark on the same side when at least one of them is in the zone, but not between boats on opposite tacks on a beat to windward. Rule 18.2: when boats are overlapped the outside boat gives the inside boat mark-room, and if they are overlapped when the first of them reaches the zone the outside boat at that moment gives mark-room from then on; a boat clear ahead when she reaches the zone is given mark-room by the boat clear astern, and that continues even if the overlap is later broken or a new one begins. Rule 18.3: when a boat passes head to wind from port to starboard in the zone of a mark to be left to port, rule 18.2 does not apply between her and a boat on starboard that is fetching the mark; if that boat has been on starboard since entering the zone, the boat that tacked must not make her sail above close-hauled to avoid contact, and gives her mark-room if she becomes overlapped inside. S7 World Sailing, The Racing Rules of Sailing for 2025-2028, with changes and corrections to April 2026. Rule 1.1: a boat or competitor gives all possible help to any person or vessel in danger. Rule 3: the decision to race, or to keep racing, is the boat's alone. Rule 41(a): a boat may be helped when a crew member is ill, injured or in danger. Rule 61.4(b)(4): a boat whose score was made significantly worse because she gave that help may ask for redress. Rule 21.3: a boat moving backwards, or sideways to windward, by backing her sail keeps clear of any boat that is not. Definition keep clear: a boat keeps clear of a right-of-way boat if the right-of-way boat can sail her course with no need to take avoiding action and, when the boats are overlapped, the right-of-way boat can also change course in both directions without immediately making contact. Rule 12: when boats are on the same tack and not overlapped, the boat clear astern keeps clear of the boat clear ahead. Rule 13, second part: when two boats are tacking at the same time, the one on the other's port side, or the one astern, keeps clear. Rule 14: a boat shall avoid contact with another boat if reasonably possible; a right-of-way boat need not act to avoid contact until it is clear that the other boat is not keeping clear, and under rule 43.1(c) she is exonerated for breaking rule 14 unless the contact causes damage or injury. Rule 17: a boat that becomes overlapped to leeward from clear astern, within two of her hull lengths of the other boat on the same tack, shall not sail above her proper course while they remain overlapped and within that distance, unless she promptly sails astern of the other boat; the rule applies only to a boat that became overlapped from clear astern, so not to one that completes a tack to leeward of the other boat; before the starting signal a boat has no proper course, so the limit does not apply then. Rule 16.1: when a right-of-way boat changes course she gives the other boat room to keep clear. Rule 31: while racing a boat shall not touch a mark. Rule 44.1 and 44.2: a boat that may have broken a rule of Part 2 in an incident while racing may take a Two-Turns Penalty, and a boat that may have broken rule 31 a One-Turn Penalty; she gets well clear of other boats as soon after the incident as possible and promptly makes the turns in the same direction, each turn including one tack and one gybe; under rule 44.1(b), if she caused injury or serious damage, or gained a significant advantage despite the turns, her penalty is to retire. Rule 60.2(a)(1): a boat that intends to protest about an incident hails Protest at the first reasonable opportunity.

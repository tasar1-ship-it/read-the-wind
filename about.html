How this book works

This book is about the start and the first beat: where the wind is, which way it is turning, where to start, and how to reach the first mark ahead. Your speed is already good. This book is about choices.

Read one chapter at a time, and try its ideas on the water before you read the next one. The chapters build on each other, so they work best in order.

What the dotted lines mark

A word with a dotted line under it has an explanation behind it. Tap it and a small panel tells you what the word means, and, where there is one, the word the coach uses for it in Spanish. All the words are also on the page called What the words mean.

A small gold button that says why sits after some sentences. Tap it for the reason behind the idea. If a panel still does not make sense, tap Still not clear and say what would help.

The pictures

Most sections have a picture, and many of them move. The wind arrow is always at the top, and the mark is always right under it. When the wind shifts, the words next to the arrow say which way. Blue is the wind, green is the good route, and amber means careful.

On the water

Every section ends with a green box. It gives you one thing to try, and how you can tell you have got it. The test is always something you can see or say on the water, not a feeling.

The blue tags

A small blue tag such as S1 means the sentence comes from the Racing Rules of Sailing. Touch it to see the rule and a link to it. Everything without a tag is sailing theory, and you can test it yourself.

Marking as you read

Select any words with your finger and four buttons appear: Highlight, Note, Ask and Copy. Ask sends a question about that passage. Everything you mark stays on this iPad or phone until you open Marks and press Email to Dad. The email is already written; you tap send.

Press Back up on the Marks page after reading. It keeps a copy of all your marks in the Files app. If your marks ever go missing, Put back brings them back from that copy, or from any email you sent Dad.
